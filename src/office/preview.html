The office

The whole floor plan at its real size, 512×352: one open floor, with only the floors and the furniture marking the areas. Along the back wall: Demiurge (four desks), the hall with the front door, Duck's glass office with the standup table in front of the boss, and the lounge down the right side with the TV, the kicker and the D&D table. Below: the open space (eight desks, two of them spare for borrowed workers), TinyWizardRoyale (two desks) under Duck's office, and under that the meeting room, where you can pitch an idea to whoever is free, and the kitchen. Everyone is at their home desk; Ada needs you. Hover a worker to see who it is.

Duck, the boss

A huge rubber duck in sunglasses and a red bow tie, in a glass office, three workers wide. Idle, it bobs, and the light glints across its shades; thinking, it sways; speaking, its beak moves; and when autopilot acts on a session, it waddles over to that desk.
